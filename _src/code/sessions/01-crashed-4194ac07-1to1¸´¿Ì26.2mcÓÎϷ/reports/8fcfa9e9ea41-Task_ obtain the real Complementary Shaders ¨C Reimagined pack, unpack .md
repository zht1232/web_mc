## Port spec (ordered)

1. **Shadow map geometry.** 2048² depth (`common.glsl:496`), PSSM-style *fractal* distortion `d = 1 − 25.6/shadowDistance` (0.8667) applied in the vertex stage (`program/shadow.glsl:348-352`) and mirrored on sample (`lib/lighting/shadowSampling.glsl:1-8`); `z *= 0.2` to buy precision; no texel snapping — flicker is instead handled by interleaved-gradient noise + TAA. Closes to `renderer.ts:535 renderShadowMap` (currently un-distorted ortho, radius 86) and `CHUNK_FS.sunShadow`. Effect: crisp contact shadows near player, soft far, stable. **M**
2. **Filter.** Hardware-filtered `shadow2D` over **two** depth buffers (tex0 opaque / tex1 all) with colored translucent shadows from `shadowcolor0`; per-quality tap count 2/4/8/16 → `2×N` symmetric spiral taps `offsetDist = (cos/sin(fract(x·2.427)·π))·1.4x/s` (`shadowSampling.glsl:39-66`), penumbra widening `offset *= 1+rainFactor2` ×4 (`:101-107`), default offset 0.00098 (`mainLighting.glsl:155-163`). **WebGL2 has no `shadow2D`** → set `TEXTURE_COMPARE_MODE = COMPARE_REF_TO_TEXTURE` + `LINEAR` on a `DEPTH_COMPONENT24` texture to get the same free 2×2 hardware PCF, then loop taps with `texture()`. Distance-weighted color: `colorMult = 2.5+5.5·pow1_5(lmY)+2.0·lmY²`, `colorPow = mix(1.5 (+0.5 above water), 0.5, lmY⁴)` (`:117-118`). **M**
3. **Bias + leak suppression.** `bias = geoNormal·(0.12 + 0.0008·|playerPos|²·^0.75)·(2 − 0.95·NdotL)` and voxel-centering `mix(playerPos, floor(p+cam+ŉ·0.01)−cam+0.5, 0.2·(1−centerFactor⁴))` (`mainLighting.glsl:168-221`), plus a **fake variable penumbra** from `dFdx(glColor.a)` gradient vs sun azimuth (`:180-193`). Replaces our `polygonOffset(1.2,2.5)` + `0.08` normal offset. Effect: no peter-panning, much less cave leaking. **M**
4. **Sun fade.** `shadowLength = shadowDistance·0.9166667 − lViewPos`; last 16 blocks lerp to `skyLightShadowMult = lmY⁸` (`mainLighting.glsl:152, 276-300`). **S**
5. **Sun BRDF.** Diffuse is **wrapped Lambert**: `NdotLM = max0(NdotL+0.4)·0.714` (`SIDE_SHADOWING` forced on, `common.glsl:362`; `mainLighting.glsl:137-146`), times `shadowTime = ((|sunVis−0.5|·2)²)²` (`gbuffers_terrain.glsl:69-71`). Specular is GGX (`lib/lighting/ggx.glsl:34-59`): `roughness = ((1.35 − sqrt1(0.9·s+0.1))²)²`, Blinn half-vector, `D = r/(π(1+(r−1)·NoH²)²)`, Schlick `F = 2^(−5.55473·LdH−6.98316)·LdH·(1−f0)+f0`, `f0=0.05`, energy-compromise `spec/(0.125·spec+1)`, `NdotL` shaped as `cbrt(NdotL·max0(dot(geoN, L)))`. Sun is an **area light** only at WATER_REFLECT≥2 (HZD `GetNoHSquared`, `ggx.glsl:1-32`). Lands in `CHUNK_FS` (`renderer.ts:109-115`). Effect: soft rounded terminator instead of our binary hard shadow term. **M**
6. **Sun colour over the day.** `lightColor = mix(clear, rain, rainFactor)`, `clear = mix(night, day, sunVisibility2)`, `day = mix(sunset, noon, noonFactor)` (`lib/colors/lightAndAmbientColors.glsl:56-78`): noon `(1.33,1.13,0.77)`, sunset `pow(0.64,0.45,0.3)^(1.5+invNoon)·5`, night `0.9·(0.15,0.14,0.20)·(0.4+0.4·vsBright)`; ambient = `pow(skyColor,0.75)·0.85` noon, `(0.081,0.108,0.153)·(1.55+0.77·vsBright)` night. Note the **non-linear day clock** `timeAngle` (`common.glsl:718-727`) and `noonFactor = sqrt(max(sin(2π·timeAngle),0))` (:741-742). Replace `renderer.ts:662-676`. **S/M**
7. **Blocklight.** Two-term curve `lightmapXM = pow((lmX⁴)^(2.8−0.6·vb+XLIGHT_CURVE) + lmX·(2.8+0.6·vb−XLIGHT_CURVE), 2.25)` (`mainLighting.glsl:349-355`), ×`blocklightCol` ×`XLIGHT_I`; held-light `pow2(pow2(held·0.47/(len+6)))` (`heldLighting.glsl:33-37`); dimmed by daylight `blockLighting *= pow(lmX/60+0.001, 0.09·lxFactor)` (:460-465). Our `curve(l)=l/(4−3l)` (`renderer.ts:77`) is far too linear — this is the single biggest torchlight gap. **S**
8. **Final mix.** `finalDiffuse = (directionShade·vanillaAO)² · (blockLighting + (sceneLighting)² + minLighting) + emission²`, then `sqrt` (`mainLighting.glsl:598-600`), where `sceneLighting = lightColor·shadowMult + ambientColor·ambientMult`; face shading is `directionShade = (0.75+0.25·NdotU)·(1−0.1·|NdotE|²)·(1+0.075·|NdotN|)` + fake bounced `ambient → lightColor` at `0.05·|NdotN|·lmY⁴` (:500-531); vanilla AO `glColor.a+0.08`, double-pow by sun angle, then `0.9+0.1` floor (:546-577); cave floor `vec3(0.005625+0.043·vsBright)·(0.45,0.475,0.6)·(1−lmY)` (`minimumLighting.glsl:3-9`). Our `aShade` (FACE_SHADE) and `aAo` map directly onto these. **M**
9. **SSAO.** Half-res? No — full-res, 4 iterations reading `depthtex`, offsets `pow2((cos,sin(fract(x·1.414)·π))·x/s)`, mirrored pairs (8 taps), linear-depth-difference → `angle+dist` clamped, `ao^(SSAO_I·0.004)` = `ao^0.4`, skipped for `z<0.56` (sky) (`program/deferred1.glsl:58-105`). Needs a scene depth texture in `postfx.ts` (we already have `depthTex` — expose it). **M**
10. **Bloom.** Not a threshold+blur: a **mipmap-tile atlas** — 7 tiles at lod 2…8 packed at fixed offsets, each a 7×7 binomial kernel `(1,6,15,20,15,6,1)²/4096`, pre-curving `pow(x/128, 0.25)` (`program/composite4.glsl:22-45`); the upscale pass squares **four times** (`bloom*=bloom; bloom*=bloom`) then `×128`, sums 7 tiles `×0.14`, and **mixes** (not adds) `color = mix(color, blur, BLOOM_STRENGTH + 0.2·darknessFactor)` at 0.12 (`program/composite5.glsl:101-145`). Replace `postfx.ts:12-38, 167-191`. WebGL2 caveat: `generateMipmap` on float targets is unreliable → build the chain with explicit half-resolution FBO passes. **M**
11. **Tonemap + grade.** **Modified Lottes 2016**, not ACES (`composite5.glsl:36-82`): `a=TM_CONTRAST=1.05, d=1, hdrMax=8, midIn=midOut=0.25`, solve `b,c`, `out = pow(x,a)/(pow(x,ad)·b+c)`, then sRGB encode `LinearToRGB`; then **dark-lift** (`mix(out, pow(x,1/2.2), 0.75·smoothstep(0.1,0,lum)·max0(0.55−|1.05−a|)/0.55)`), **white path** toward `(1,1,1)` (strength 1.0, input curve `pow(lum/16, 2−1)`), **dark desaturation** 0.25. Then BSL-style saturation/vibrance (`:84-98`, both 1.00 = no-op). **No auto-exposure exists** — `vsBrightness = clamp(screenBrightness)` is just the client gamma. Our `EXTRACT_FS`+`uExposure/uContrast/uSaturation` model must be replaced wholesale. **M**
12. **Vignette / final.** `1 − dot(q,q)·(1 − luminance)` (`final.glsl:155-159`) — luminance-aware, unlike our flat `uVignette 0.06`; plus blue-noise `+ (d−0.25)/128` dither (:161-162) and a 4-tap unsharp `color *= 1+0.05·5; color −= Σ neighbours·0.03125` (:58-77). **S**
13. **TAA + FXAA — both are ON by default.** `TAA_DEFINE −1` is overridden by `#if DETAIL_QUALITY >= 2 … #define TAA` (`common.glsl:547-551`). 8-step jitter Halton, `TAA_JITTER 1` → `×0.125` (`lib/antialiasing/jitter.glsl:2-21`); history in `RGBA16F`; Catmull-Rom history resample; 8-neighbour AABB `ClipAABB` with depth-edge detection (`|ΔlinDepth|>0.09`) raising `edge` by `regularEdge=6` at SMOOTHING 3; blend `clamp(exp(−7·|v|²)·0.2 + 0.7 − min(|Δcam|,0.05)·edge, 0.35)`. Then **FXAA 3.11** at strength 75 with motion-based skip (`FXAA_TAA_INTERACTION 10`) in `composite7.glsl:31`. Our hand-written god-ray/`rays` block in `COMPOSITE_FS` should be replaced by this order: TAA → FXAA. Effort **L** (needs history texture, `previousViewProj`, and jitter shared between the vertex stage and all screen passes) — but it is the reason Complementary's shadows don't shimmer.
14. **Water (cheap).** Default `WATER_STYLE 1` + `RAIN_PUDDLES 0` means **no normal map at all** (`translucents/water.glsl:80-120` is inside `#if WATER_STYLE >= 2`): waves are 2 sines in the **vertex** stage — `worldPos.xz·14`, `sin(t·0.7 − z·0.14 + x·0.07) + sin(t·0.5 − z·0.10 + x·0.05)`, `t = frameTime·6·WAVING_SPEED`, `wave = Σ·0.125 − 0.05`, `lmY` indoor gating, plus `normal = mix(normal, tangent, wave·0.01)` (`wavingBlocks.glsl:69-93`). Alpha from depth: `1 − exp(Δ·0.075)` → `color.a = 0.25+0.75·waterFog`, `alpha = mix(α,1,fresnel⁴)`; foam only when `NdotU>0.99` near shallow depth (`water.glsl:210-250`). **Move this to S→M — it makes our texture-derived water normal wrong by design.**
15. **Clouds.** Yes, **volumetric ray-marched** against a procedural noise custom image: layer alt 192, `stretch 4.2`, `tallness 8.4`, `roundness 0.125`, `max(planeDistance/8, 12)` steps, **first-hit break** (single scattering, opaque), 2-sample sun self-shadow, shading `pow(1−(top−y)/8.4, 0.8)` (`reimaginedClouds.glsl:3-146`). **L**, and needs a noise texture we don't generate — recommend deferring.
16. **God rays.** `nearSamples 10`, exponential-ish `pow(t,2.0)` distribution, `maxDistance = far·0.98`, `fogCurve 0.9`, shadowmap-sampled density (`volumetricLight.glsl:88-118, 183`). Delete our `uRayStrength` cone hack and reuse the shadow map. **M**

## WebGL2 blockers

`shadow2D` → `COMPARE_REF_TO_TEXTURE` (legal, equivalent). Compute shaders / `imageStore` / SSBOs (`shadowcomp.csh`, `lib/voxelization/lightVoxelization.glsl`) → **not needed**: `COLORED_LIGHTING 0` disables them by default (`shaders.properties:110-113`). Float colour attachments → `RGBA16F` behind `EXT_color_buffer_float`; float *filtering* is core for 16F, but `generateMipmap` on float may fail → explicit downsample passes. Bindless / `gl_TextureMatrix[1]` → our mesher already carries `aLight` per-vertex (`mesher.ts:47-56`), so the lightmap path is fine; Complementary's `GetLightMapCoordinates()` re-normalisation `(lm − 0.03125)·1.06667` (`commonFunctions.glsl:11-15`) is worth mirroring since our sky/block are already 0-1. `dFdx(glColor.a)` works in ES3.

## What NOT to port

Iris/Sodium G-buffer machinery and anything reading a deferred material/smoothness/normal atlas: `terrainIPBR.glsl` (2852 lines) + `irisIPBR`/`entityIPBR`/`deferredIPBR` (labPBR), connected textures, POM parallax, anisotropic filtering, world-space reflections + `reflectionVoxelization` SSBOs, Distant Horizons/Voxy patches, aurora/nebula/rainbows/nether storm/end beams, `SELECT_OUTLINE`, `WORLD_OUTLINE`, `showLightLevels`, textRenderer error screens, `clrwl_*` colorwheel passes, custom `gaux*` cloud-noise and puddle voxel images. Also skip `COLORGRADING`, `LIGHT_COLOR_MULTS`, `ATM_COLOR_MULTS`, moon-phase influence, `PIXELATED_*` — all off by default.
